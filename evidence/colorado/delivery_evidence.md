# Colorado: approved dental care and the delivery record

The [Colorado DentaQuest FY2024–2025 compliance review](https://spl.cde.state.co.us/artemis/hcpserials/hcp62125internet/hcp62125202425internet.pdf) discusses coordination of care under Standard III. The relevant account appears in Section 2, printed pp. 2-1–2-3 (PDF pp. 5–7).

DentaQuest supplied a written procedure for coordinating care. During the review, the auditor did not receive evidence that the procedure was operating as written. The report also described an earlier FY2021–2022 corrective-action plan that called for care-coordination procedures.

Within the tracking records, the auditor identified a member whose extra dental cleanings had been approved. The approval had not been recorded in the member's chart or communicated to the dental provider. According to the report, the member could not receive the additional cleanings and filed a billing grievance with the Colorado department.

| Step | What the audit records | Useful follow-up record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service authorization | Extra cleanings approved | Dated authorization and scope of approved services |
| Member chart | Approval absent from the chart | Chart entry and transmission history |
| Provider notification | Dental provider had not received the approval | Provider message and receipt confirmation |
| Access to care | Member could not obtain the extra cleanings at that point | Appointment, claims, and service-delivery records |
| Grievance | Member reported the problem to the department | Complaint handling and resolution records |
| Later outcome | The cited account gives no confirmed later delivery date | Subsequent claims, visits, or member confirmation |

The report describes a separate FY2023–2024 corrective action as approved and completed in October 2024 (Section 4). That action concerns a different obligation and is kept apart from the earlier coordination issue.

This example concerns Colorado's CHP+ dental program. The Louisiana contract review and the Nevada retrieval tests use different programs and records. The case is included to show the documentary steps needed to follow an administrative approval through to a service.
