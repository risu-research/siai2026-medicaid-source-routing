# MCNA contract trail: documents relevant to 2024

The 2025 external quality review assessed MCNA Dental's grievance-and-appeal materials for calendar year 2024. To evaluate which instruction governed a particular correction, the relevant contract amendments need to be read in their effective sequence. This page records official documents located so far. It is an inventory for further legal research, with a [CSV copy](contract_amendment_trail.csv) for reuse.

The amendment's stated effective date and the execution or state-approval date are separate fields. For Amendment 12 and Amendment 13, the date printed for the change precedes the final approval date. This inventory records those dates as found in the available sources and leaves their legal implications for direct review of the agreements.

The [March 12, 2026 monetary-penalty notice](enforcement_followup_2026.md) refers to a contract effective January 1, 2023. Amendment 10, meanwhile, describes an extension of the agreement that began in 2021. The record needs another check before drawing a conclusion about how those descriptions relate.

3. Record page locations, actual replacement text, effective dates, and relevant approval dates. An amendment heading alone cannot establish which paragraph applied.
4. Compare the operative clauses with the precise items cited by the compliance review. Keep agency audit findings, governing rules, and the later enforcement notice in separate columns.

The earlier paper analyzed available passages from the 2021 original contract, Amendment 2, Amendments 11–12, and the later 2026 agreement. This trail adds located intermediate amendments. No conclusion is drawn here that the complete 2024 contract chain has been authenticated.
