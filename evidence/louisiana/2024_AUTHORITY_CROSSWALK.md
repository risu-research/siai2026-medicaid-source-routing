# MCNA appeals: sources applicable to the 2024 review

This research record follows the rules at issue in the Louisiana Department of Health's 2025 compliance review of MCNA Dental, which assessed calendar-year 2024 records. It draws from the federal regulations as published in the **October 1, 2024 CFR edition**, the original executed contract, contract amendments, and HSAG's specific findings.

For each issue, the governing document, what it actually says, and the policy correction requested by the audit are shown separately. The [CSV](2024_authority_crosswalk.csv) contains the same source references for further research.

## 1. Oral appeals: an old handbook instruction

**Federal rule.** [42 CFR § 438.402(c)(3)(ii), 2024 edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title42-vol4/pdf/CFR-2024-title42-vol4-part438.pdf) permits appeal requests orally or in writing. Section 438.406(b)(3) requires oral inquiries seeking an appeal to be treated as appeals. The 2020 federal revision removed the former written-follow-up requirement; see [CMS's Medicaid managed care contract checklist](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/downloads/mce-checklist-state-user-guide.pdf), items I.H.6.01 and I.H.6.03.

**Contract history.** The [2021 executed agreement](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf), § 2.10.3.1.2 (PDF pp. **94–95**, printed pp. 87–88), had required a signed confirmation within 15 days and permitted closure of the appeal if the form was missing. [Amendment 2](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd2MCNA.pdf), Attachment B-2 item 9 (PDF pp. 8–9), removed that member obligation and preserved the plan's appeal-documentation responsibility.

**What HSAG found.** Audit items **13** (printed p. B-246) and **17** (printed p. B-250) identify a **written follow-up** requirement in the member handbooks. Under item 13, the action was to remove that wording from the Adult Denture and EPSDT handbooks. Item 17 addressed the Adult Denture handbook's treatment of oral inquiries as appeals. The audit describes a written follow-up requirement; the earlier signed form is identifiable in the **superseded original contract**. Those are distinct documentary facts.

## 2. State fair hearing: the event that starts the clock

**Federal rule.** [42 CFR § 438.408(f)(2), 2024 edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title42-vol4/pdf/CFR-2024-title42-vol4-part438.pdf) allowed the state to set a filing period of **90–120 calendar days** measured from the plan's **appeal-resolution notice**.

**Contract history.** [Amendment 2](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd2MCNA.pdf), Attachment B-2 item 10 (PDF p. 9), stated **120 calendar days** after receipt of the appeal-resolution notice in § 2.10.6.1. The original contract contained the earlier § 2.10.6.1 (original PDF p. 97).

**What HSAG found.** Item **28** (printed pp. B-261–B-262) found that two policies gave the deadline as 90–120 days from the initial adverse-benefit-determination (ABD) notice. The internal **GA Job Aid Louisiana Appeals and SFH** already used the correct appeal-resolution event. The required revision was to make the two policies say **120 calendar days from the appeal-resolution notice**, matching the state contract and the audit's selected deadline.

## 3. Continued benefits: when they begin and when they end

The two federal subsections serve different purposes.

| Source | Stage and function | Textual structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [42 CFR § 438.420(b)(4), 2024 edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title42-vol4/pdf/CFR-2024-title42-vol4-part438.pdf) | Qualifying for continued benefits | The original authorization period has not expired |
| [42 CFR § 438.420(c)(1)–(3), 2024 edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title42-vol4/pdf/CFR-2024-title42-vol4-part438.pdf) | Duration after continuation has begun | Three specified ending circumstances |
| [2021 MCNA agreement § 2.10.3.5.1–.5.4](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf), PDF p. **96** (printed p. 89) | Ending continued benefits during plan appeal | Four listed circumstances, including the authorized service period or limit being reached |
| [HSAG audit item 29](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/EQRO/2025/Compliance/MCNA_LA_2025_CR_Report_F1.pdf), printed p. B-262 | Whether continuation begins | **Met** |
| [HSAG audit item 30](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/EQRO/2025/Compliance/MCNA_LA_2025_CR_Report_F1.pdf), printed pp. B-263–B-264 | Policy statement on ending continuation | **Not Met**; HSAG requested the time/service-limit clause |

The audit's item 30 cites **42 CFR § 438.420(c)** along with **PAHP contract §§ 2.10.3.5 and 2.10.6.9**. The additional time/service-limit event is presented under the **MCO/PAHP appeals** part of the audit requirement. The 2024 federal § 438.420(c) lists three events; the extra fourth appears in the original MCNA contract. Section 438.420(b)(4) separately addresses whether the original authorization had already expired when continued benefits are requested.

This source distinction matters when describing an audit correction. An instruction to add the contract's fourth clause does not itself decide whether a member's continued coverage was lawfully ended. That would require the applicable full contract history, procedural stage, and member record. The audit's process description reported no examples of member continuation requests.

## 4. Which contract is meant by “effective January 1, 2023”?

The official records use different descriptions of the agreement.

- The [LDH Dental Services page](https://ldh.la.gov/medicaid/dental-services) says MCNA and DentaQuest began their contracts **January 1, 2021**.
- The [original executed contract](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf) gives a **January 1, 2021** beginning date.
- [Amendment 10](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd10MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf) extended the existing contract term through **December 31, 2025**, including the 2024 audit period.
- The [March 12, 2026 penalty notice](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/docs/BayouHealth/Accountability/2.0/MCNA/MCNA1-16_Notice_of_Monetary_Penalty-_2025_External_Quality_Compliance_Review_3.12.26.pdf), p. 1, describes the contract governing CY2024 as effective **January 1, 2023**.

The penalty notice does not identify a separate executed 2023 dental contract in the text reviewed here. The other documents establish the original start and the extension of agreement no. **2000506243**. This is a source-description discrepancy, recorded for direct follow-up. It would be premature to label the notice a clerical error or to claim another contract was signed in 2023.

## 5. What the documents establish for 2024

The contract text and the audit provide strong document-level evidence of the original oral-appeal requirement, its deletion in Amendment 2, the corrected hearing clock, and the contractual fourth continued-benefits event. [Amendment 9](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd9MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf), Attachment B9 item 7, separately revised the grievance extension and notification steps effective May 2023. [Amendment 11](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd11MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf) added an appeal-reversal threshold effective January 2024.

The executed amendment inventory covers the original agreement and Amendments 1–13. It has not been certified as a consolidated clause-by-clause legal text for every date in 2024. The identified changed sections and their page references are in the [clause-history record](CLAUSE_HISTORY_2024.md). The remaining documentary question is whether any amendment or incorporated state instrument changed these particular provisions before the audit period. The notice, later penalty, approved CAP, and member-level remedy are separate records with separate evidence requirements.
