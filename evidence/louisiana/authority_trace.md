# Louisiana: following the governing text

The [2025 MCNA compliance review](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/EQRO/2025/Compliance/MCNA_LA_2025_CR_Report_F1.pdf) assessed calendar-year 2024 grievance-and-appeal materials. Standard XIII contained 38 elements: 31 Met, six Not Met, and one Not Applicable. The six findings appear individually in [the evidence ledger](correction_ledger.csv). Items 13 and 17 address one recurring handbook problem, leaving five topics for review.

The ledger records a proposed response to each finding. The categories MOVE, REMOVE, ADD_FOR_REVIEW, and CHECK were assigned in this study. They are separate from HSAG's official scoring.

## Hearing deadline: item 28

Two submitted policies counted the state-fair-hearing period from the initial adverse-benefit notice. The audit identified the later appeal-resolution notice as the starting point and found that the staff job aid already used it. The filing period was 120 calendar days.

- [MCNA compliance review](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/EQRO/2025/Compliance/MCNA_LA_2025_CR_Report_F1.pdf), Standard XIII, item 28, printed pp. B-260–B-262.
- [Amendment 2](https://www.ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd2MCNA.pdf), Attachment B-2, item 10, on the hearing filing period.
- [42 C.F.R. § 438.408(f)(2), 2024 edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CFR-2024-title42-vol4/CFR-2024-title42-vol4-sec438-408).

The proposed correction changes the two policies' starting event and retains the 120-day period and accurate job aid. Copies of the revised policies would document the edit. Member notices and hearing files would help trace its use in practice.

## Oral appeals: items 13 and 17

The audited handbooks directed members who appealed by phone to submit a signed written follow-up. The [original MCNA contract](https://www.ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243MCNA-ApprovedContractPacket.pdf) at § 2.10.3.1.2 required a signed confirmation within 15 days and permitted closure when the form was missing. [Amendment 2](https://www.ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/ContractsDental/2000506243Amd2MCNA.pdf), Attachment B-2, item 9, removed those provisions. Its stated effective date was January 1, 2021, with state approval dated June 25, 2021.

A handbook correction would remove the member's extra submission step. The plan's own appeal documentation and any applicable authorization rules for representatives remain separate matters. The audit and amendment show a difference between the governing text and the reviewed instructions. The number of members who received those instructions remains unverified.

## Continued benefits: item 30

The audit found four events listed in the submitted process description for ending continued benefits. Policy 13.209 contained three. One missing event concerned the end of an approved time or service limit.

For the legal check, the source dates matter:

| [2024 federal regulation](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CFR-2024-title42-vol4/CFR-2024-title42-vol4-sec438-420) | 42 C.F.R. § 438.420(b)(4), (c) | Conditions for continuation and events ending it, addressed in separate provisions |

The full amendment chain governing the audited 2024 period has not been assembled. A proposed addition to policy 13.209 should therefore carry the applicable agreement and appeal stage for legal review. An individual member's continued coverage also depends on the case record.

## Records and participants: items 6 and 37

Under item 6, two submitted grievance files lacked resolution notices. The next check is the notice and delivery record for each file. Item 37 concerns how member materials describe participants in appeals and fair hearings. Revised materials would address the identified wording. Distribution and participation records would show how those materials were used.

## Further records

The public audit report describes the materials HSAG reviewed. The submitted policies, complete 2024 amended agreement, completed corrective-action plan, and member-level follow-up records remain useful targets for subsequent document review.
